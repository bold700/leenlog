# Operoo — change requests from meeting 28-08-2026 · delivery 14 September

Source: client meeting notes, written out 28 Aug. Uncertain readings marked with (?) — to be confirmed with the client.

## 1. Projects (new concept)
- Create a new project with: address, customer (select from list) and contact person (name + mobile number), with a plus button to add an extra contact person
- All work orders within a project automatically inherit the customer + contact person
- A project has its own **project notes** and **underlying work orders**
- Comments/notes are shown **as a timeline**

## 2. Projects overview
- Project name as its own column
- PDF upload per project, visible from all work orders in that project
- Filter on projects (?) — to confirm

## 3. Planning
- Make multi-day tasks visually clear in the planning view
- Show the project name in the planning view

## 4. Tasks and progress (biggest change)
- A task is currently only a checkbox (done/not done); this must become **progress logging in units**. Example: 100 metres total; Monday 10/100, Tuesday 30/100, Wednesday 50/100, with per day who and how much (?)
- Send a **notification** when progress is logged

## 5. Stock and materials (on the work order)
- Register: **handed out** to the fitter vs. **actually used** vs. **what comes back**
- Link to the packing slip, with a checkbox "received"
- Materials can also be **delivered on location** (?); in that case the work order must still register what comes back
- Ability to **add new materials**, including other types of **products and services**

## 5b. Sales and data
- Insight into **sales data**: what has been sold (based on work orders/materials)

## 6. Permissions and accounts
- A fitter only sees **his own assigned projects and work orders**
- Ability to **create new accounts** (admin)

## 7. Testing
- Test the **fitter view** with a real fitter account before delivery
